Cheat Sheet · Tenses (2/2)

EN + ES · most common first

6 Future with going to Futuro con going to

Use: Plans decided before speaking; predictions based on evidence.
Planes ya decididos; predicciones con evidencia.
+am / is / are going to + verb
−am / is / are not going to + verb
?Are you going to + verb?
Signals: next month, this year, soon, tonight
We're going to launch in May.Vamos a lanzar en mayo.
I'm going to update the plan.Voy a actualizar el plan.
Are you going to attend the demo?¿Vas a asistir a la demo?
We're going to miss the deadline.Vamos a incumplir el plazo (se ve por los retrasos).
💡 Going to = plan already made · will = decision made now.

7 Past Continuous Pasado continuo

Use: Action in progress at a past moment; an action interrupted by another; background.
Acción en curso en el pasado; acción interrumpida.
+was / were + verb-ing
−wasn't / weren't + verb-ing
?Was / Were + subject + verb-ing?
Signals: while, when, as, at 5 pm yesterday, all morning
I was testing when the server crashed.Estaba probando cuando el servidor cayó.
We were discussing the budget.Estábamos hablando del presupuesto.
What were you doing at 3 p.m.?¿Qué estabas haciendo a las 3?
While I was presenting, my connection dropped.Mientras presentaba, se cortó mi conexión.
💡 Long action = past continuous · short interruption = past simple.

8 Present Perfect Continuous Perfecto continuo

Use: Started in the past and still going; recent activity; stresses duration.
Empezó en el pasado y continúa; énfasis en la duración.
+have / has been + verb-ing
−haven't / hasn't been + verb-ing
?Have / Has + subject + been + verb-ing?
Signals: for, since, all day, lately, recently, how long
I've been working on this for two weeks.Llevo dos semanas trabajando en esto.
How long have you been waiting?¿Cuánto tiempo llevas esperando?
We've been using it since January.Lo usamos desde enero.
She's been testing all morning.Lleva toda la mañana probando.
💡 for + duration (for two weeks) · since + starting point (since Monday).

9 Past Perfect Pluscuamperfecto

Use: Action completed before another past action or time; explaining reasons.
Acción anterior a otra en el pasado; explicar causas.
+had + past participle
−hadn't + past participle
?Had + subject + past participle?
Signals: before, after, by the time, already, just, until
The call had already started when I joined.La llamada ya había empezado cuando me conecté.
I hadn't seen the update.No había visto la actualización.
By the time we arrived, they had left.Cuando llegamos, ya se habían ido.
Had you read the document before the meeting?¿Habías leído el documento antes de la reunión?
💡 Use it with past simple to show which event came first.

10 Conditionals 1 & 2 Condicionales

Use: 1st: real or likely situations. 2nd: hypothetical, unlikely, polite suggestions.
1º: situaciones reales. 2º: hipotéticas o educadas.
1stIf + present simple, will + verb
2ndIf + past simple, would + verb
?What will / would you do if...?
Signals: if, unless, as long as, in case, what if
If we finish early, we'll release on Friday.Si terminamos pronto, lanzaremos el viernes.
If I had more time, I would help.Si tuviera más tiempo, ayudaría.
Unless we get approval, we can't start.A menos que nos aprueben, no podemos empezar.
It would be great if you could send it.Sería genial que pudieras enviarlo.
💡 Polite: "Could you...?", "I'd suggest...", "If I were you, I'd...".
