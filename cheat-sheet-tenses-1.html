Cheat Sheet · Tenses (1/2)

EN + ES · most common first

1 Present Simple Presente simple

Use: Routines and habits, facts, fixed schedules.
Rutinas, hechos y horarios fijos.
+I/you/we/they work · he/she/it works
−don't / doesn't + verb
?Do / Does + subject + verb?
Signals: always, usually, often, never, every day, on Mondays
We have a stand-up every morning.Tenemos una reunión rápida cada mañana.
The deployment starts at 10 a.m.El despliegue empieza a las 10.
She doesn't work on Fridays.Ella no trabaja los viernes.
Does this make sense?¿Esto tiene sentido?
💡 Don't forget the -s in he/she/it: "He works", not "He work". / No olvides la -s.

2 Present Continuous Presente continuo

Use: Happening now, temporary or current projects, arranged plans.
Ahora mismo, proyectos actuales, planes acordados.
+am / is / are + verb-ing
−am / is / are + not + verb-ing
?Are you + verb-ing?
Signals: now, at the moment, currently, this week, today
I'm working on the report.Estoy trabajando en el informe.
We're waiting for feedback.Estamos esperando comentarios.
Are you sharing your screen?¿Estás compartiendo la pantalla?
We're meeting the client tomorrow.Nos reunimos con el cliente mañana (plan acordado).
💡 State verbs (know, understand, want, need) don't take -ing: "I understand", not "I'm understanding".

3 Past Simple Pasado simple

Use: Finished actions at a specific past time; a sequence of past events.
Acciones terminadas en un momento concreto del pasado.
+verb-ed / irregular (went, sent, had)
−didn't + base verb
?Did + subject + base verb?
Signals: yesterday, last week, two days ago, in 2025, when
We finished the test yesterday.Terminamos la prueba ayer.
I didn't receive your email.No recibí tu correo.
Did you send the invoice?¿Enviaste la factura?
The client called and asked for a change.El cliente llamó y pidió un cambio.
💡 After did / didn't use the base verb: "Did you send", not "Did you sent".

4 Present Perfect Pretérito perfecto

Use: Past action with a present result, experience, unfinished periods (this week).
Pasado con relevancia actual; experiencias.
+have / has + past participle
−haven't / hasn't + past participle
?Have / Has + subject + p.p.?
Signals: already, yet, just, ever, never, so far, recently
I've sent you the summary.Te he enviado el resumen.
We haven't decided yet.Todavía no hemos decidido.
Have you ever used this tool?¿Has usado alguna vez esta herramienta?
She's just joined the team.Acaba de unirse al equipo.
💡 Not with a finished time: "I sent it yesterday", not "I have sent it yesterday".

5 Future with will Futuro con will

Use: Decisions made now, promises and offers, predictions or opinions.
Decisiones en el momento, promesas, ofrecimientos, predicciones.
+will + base verb (I'll call)
−won't + base verb
?Will + subject + verb?
Signals: probably, I think, I'm sure, tomorrow, soon, later
I'll send the minutes today.Enviaré el acta hoy.
I'll check and get back to you.Lo reviso y te respondo.
It won't take long.No tardará mucho.
Will you be available on Friday?¿Estarás disponible el viernes?
💡 Perfect for offers in meetings: "I'll help you with that." / "I'll take care of it."
