Cheat Sheet · General Phrases

Keep it short, speak slowly

1 Opening & greetings

  • Good morning, everyone. Thanks for joining.
  • Let's get started / Shall we begin?
  • The goal of today's meeting is to…
  • We have 30 minutes, so let's be efficient.
  • Let's go through the agenda.
  • Can everyone hear me / see my screen?

2 Giving your opinion

  • In my opinion… / I think that…
  • From my point of view…
  • I'd suggest / I'd recommend…
  • It seems to me that…
  • My main concern is…
  • I strongly believe we should…

3 Agreeing & disagreeing

  • ＋ I totally agree. / Exactly. / That makes sense.
  • ＋ I'm on the same page.
  • ± I see your point, but…
  • ± That's a fair point, however…
  • － I'm afraid I disagree.
  • － I'm not sure that would work because…

4 Asking for clarification

  • Could you clarify what you mean by…?
  • Sorry, I didn't catch that. Could you repeat it?
  • Could you speak a bit slower, please?
  • So, if I understand correctly, you're saying…
  • Just to confirm, …
  • Could you give me an example?

5 Interrupting politely

  • Sorry to interrupt, but…
  • Can I add something here?
  • May I jump in for a second?
  • Let me finish my point, please.
  • Going back to what you said earlier…
  • Let's not go off-topic. (= stay on topic)

6 Buying time

  • Let me think about that for a moment.
  • That's a good question.
  • I'll need to check and get back to you.
  • I don't have that information right now.
  • Let me look into it and follow up by email.
  • Can we come back to this later? (park it)

7 Status updates & useful meeting vocabulary

  • I'm working on… / I've completed…
  • We're on track / ahead of / behind schedule.
  • We ran into an issue with…
  • The next step is to…
  • blocker – something stopping progress
  • deadline – date when work is due
  • action item – task assigned to someone
  • stakeholder – person with interest in the project
  • to align – to agree on the same approach
  • to escalate – to pass an issue to a manager
  • bandwidth – available time/capacity
  • ETA – estimated time of arrival/completion

8 Closing the meeting

  • Let's wrap up. / To sum up…
  • Who's going to take care of…?
  • Let's set a deadline for that.
  • I'll send the minutes / a summary by email.
  • Does anyone have any final questions?
  • Thanks all. Have a great day!
