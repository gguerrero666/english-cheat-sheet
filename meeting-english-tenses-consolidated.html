Meeting English · Tenses

Support sheet v1 · most common first · EN + ES

1 Present Simple Presente simple

Routines, facts, schedules · Rutinas, hechos, horarios
I/you/we/they work · he/she/it works
The meeting starts at 10.La reunión empieza a las 10.
I don't have that information.No tengo esa información.
Signals: always, usually, every day, never

2 Present Continuous Presente continuo

Happening now, current projects, near plans · Ahora, proyectos actuales
am/is/are + verb-ing
I'm working on the report.Estoy trabajando en el informe.
We're waiting for feedback.Estamos esperando comentarios.
Signals: now, at the moment, currently, this week

3 Past Simple Pasado simple

Finished actions at a specific time · Acciones terminadas
verb-ed / irregular (went, did) · didn't + verb
We finished the test yesterday.Terminamos la prueba ayer.
I didn't receive your email.No recibí tu correo.
Signals: yesterday, last week, ago, in 2025

4 Present Perfect Pretérito perfecto

Past with present result, experience · Pasado con relevancia actual
have/has + past participle
I've sent you the summary.Te he enviado el resumen.
We haven't decided yet.Todavía no hemos decidido.
Signals: already, yet, just, ever, never, so far

5 Future: will Futuro con will

Decisions now, promises, predictions · Decisiones, promesas
will + verb · won't + verb
I'll send the minutes today.Enviaré el acta hoy.
I'll check and get back to you.Lo reviso y te respondo.
Signals: probably, I think, tomorrow, soon

6 Future: going to Futuro con going to

Plans and intentions · Planes e intenciones
am/is/are + going to + verb
We're going to launch in May.Vamos a lanzar en mayo.
I'm going to update the plan.Voy a actualizar el plan.
Signals: next month, this year, soon

7 Past Continuous Pasado continuo

Action in progress in the past · Acción en curso en el pasado
was/were + verb-ing
I was testing when the server crashed.Estaba probando cuando el servidor cayó.
We were discussing the budget.Estábamos hablando del presupuesto.
Signals: while, when, at 5 pm yesterday

8 Present Perfect Continuous Perfecto continuo

Started in the past, still going · Empezó antes y continúa
have/has been + verb-ing
I've been working on this for two weeks.Llevo dos semanas trabajando en esto.
How long have you been waiting?¿Cuánto llevas esperando?
Signals: for, since, all day, lately

9 Past Perfect Pluscuamperfecto

Action before another past action · Anterior a otro pasado
had + past participle
The call had already started when I joined.La llamada ya había empezado cuando me conecté.
I hadn't seen the update.No había visto la actualización.
Signals: before, by the time, already, after

10 Conditionals 1 & 2 Condicionales

Real vs. hypothetical situations · Reales vs. hipotéticas
If + present, will · If + past, would
If we finish early, we'll release on Friday.Si terminamos pronto, lanzaremos el viernes.
If I had more time, I would help.Si tuviera más tiempo, ayudaría.
Tip: "I'd suggest…" / "It would be great if…" sound polite
